# Multiplayer systems on Meridian

- **Sabotage.** The impostor’s map shows system and shutter controls. Lights use a shared switch bank. Comms uses a tuning dial and disables Security and task information. Oxygen needs a local code at each station. Reactor requires two different players at its stations within the same 5.2-second window. Both critical alarms have a 40-second deadline. The emergency console locks during system sabotage. Reporting a body can still start a meeting before the deadline and clears the alarm.
- **Hatches.** Use V or the hatch controls when a permitted role is close enough. The map shows connected destinations. Engineers use a limited travel window and a cooldown. Living crew cleaning a hatch eject its occupant and block entry while cleaning.
- **Security.** The Security console streams four fixed views of the west passage, Engineering, Admin, and Navigation. Only active watchers receive those views. They show visible suit movement without nicknames, and omit players hidden in hatches or by active abilities. Nearby players can see the camera indicator while the console is active.
- **Controls and settings.** Hold Tab during play to peek at the map, or use M to toggle it. The relay enforces the captain’s match rules across rounds. Discussion and voting keep separate timers. The task bar can update continuously, during meetings, or stay hidden.

## Multiplayer match fixes

- The captain explicitly saves the lobby rules before launch. The client displays the relay’s saved settings and calls out invalid fields, including fields inside a collapsed role section. The lobby shows the common, long, and short quotas and their per-player sum. The minimum is four connected players with one impostor, six with two, and eight with three.
- Each round uses one shared selection of common tasks and draws individual long and short tasks. A task with multiple stops adds one completion after its last stage. The maintenance list gives personal progress. The HUD counts only crew assignments, including tasks still owed by crew ghosts. Zero assigned tasks leaves sabotage and ejection as the win conditions. [Innersloth glossary](https://innersloth.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/7916545454868-Among-Us-Glossary) · [Innersloth notes on wiring stages](https://www.innersloth.com/balance-tweaks-in-among-us-2018-10-29/)
- A unique player with the most votes is ejected. A winning Skip, a tie, or an empty ballot leaves everyone aboard. Uncast votes expire as abstentions. Votes for a candidate who is offline when voting closes are dropped. A submitted ballot can survive a temporary disconnect during its reconnect window, and reconnecting never grants another ballot. The optional Judge can override a verdict when enabled and assigned. The result marks overrides while retaining the actual tally. [Innersloth voting notes](https://www.innersloth.com/balance-tweaks-in-among-us-2018-10-29/) · [Innersloth Judge rules](https://www.innersloth.com/new-crewmate-role-the-judge-emergency-meeting-43/)

## Fayez Among Us release

- All optional roles start disabled. Settings group the match rules, tasks, meetings, Crewmates, Impostors, and Neutrals. The host uses predefined values and can load the recommended settings before saving. The relay validates changes as a unit and retains them for the next match. [Innersloth settings categories](https://innersloth.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/8531332682004-What-accessibility-options-are-available-in-Among-Us) · [Gameplay settings screenshot and guide](https://progameguides.com/among-us/how-to-change-custom-settings-in-among-us/)
- The picker follows the reference’s quarter-step multipliers, 2.5-second Kill Cooldown steps, 15-second discussion and voting steps, and Short/Normal/Long distance. Fayez Among Us uses a 0.75x–2.0x speed range, 0.25x–2.0x vision, a finite voting deadline, and the existing Meridian task quotas. Its recommended preset uses one Impostor, 1.25x speed, 1.0x/1.5x vision, 20-second Kill Cooldown, and 30/60-second discussion/voting.
- The original Jester and Vulture implementations draw on mod rules. The Jester wins through a normal ejection vote. The Vulture can privately collect only sites preserved by a successful report, after the meeting. The relay caps neutral slots at two and reserves enough seats to start with a viable Crewmate team. It also limits the Vulture goal to the number of bodies the starting lobby can produce before parity. Neutral players count as opposing votes for parity and contribute no Crewmate tasks. [The Other Roles mod](https://github.com/TheOtherRolesAU/TheOtherRoles/blob/main/README.md) · [Town of Us role ideas](https://github.com/amongusclub/Town-Of-Us-Reactivated/blob/master/README.md)
- Executioner and Survivor are potential follow-ups. An Executioner gives one player a private voting target. A Survivor can share a faction’s win by staying alive. Either needs balance testing in small private rooms.
- The original illustrated controls follow the user’s sprite-sheet reference through bold silhouettes, outlines, and readable labels. The external sprites are reference material. [Innersloth game gallery](https://innersloth.itch.io/among-us)
